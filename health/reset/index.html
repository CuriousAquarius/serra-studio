FAQ

RESET questions

Is RESET only five days?

The RESET kit is a structured five-day jump-start. After completing it, many people continue using Nutrimeal shakes as one or two meals per day as part of an ongoing weight management plan. The five days are a starting point, not the whole program.

How much weight can I lose on RESET?

Results vary by individual. RESET is designed to help you build better eating habits and create a calorie deficit over five days, not to promise a specific number on the scale. People use it as a reset point before adopting a longer-term healthy eating plan.

Can I do RESET while exercising?

Light exercise is fine during RESET. High-intensity training on 1,200 calories per day is not recommended. If you are training hard, talk to your doctor before starting any low-calorie program.

Are the shakes filling enough?

Nutrimeal is formulated to be high in protein and fiber with a low glycemic index, which means it digests slowly and keeps you feeling full longer than a typical protein shake or meal replacement. Most people find the combination of shakes and snack bars manages hunger well.
