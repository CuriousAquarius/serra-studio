How Much Does a Website Cost for a Small Business in Orange County?

May 11, 2026 7 min read Studio-Serra

For a small business in Orange County, a professional website typically costs between $500 and $3,000 to build, plus ongoing costs for hosting and maintenance. That range is nearly useless without context, so here is a proper breakdown of what you actually get at each price point.

Option 1: Build it yourself (Wix, Squarespace, GoDaddy)

Cost: $0 upfront, $16-50 per month

These platforms let anyone drag-and-drop a website together without coding. They work, but they come with real limitations for local businesses in Orange County trying to rank on Google.

  • Local SEO is minimal and generic — hard to rank for "plumber in Anaheim" vs. just "plumber"
  • You spend 10-30 hours setting it up, then it sits untouched for years
  • Mobile layout requires manual work to get right
  • No support when something breaks — you're on your own

This is the right option if you have zero budget and plenty of time. It is not the right option if you want the site to bring in leads.

Option 2: Hire a freelancer

Cost: $500-$3,000 one-time, plus $15-30/month for hosting

A freelancer builds a custom website for you. The quality varies enormously depending on who you hire. On the good end, you get a polished site tailored to your business. On the bad end, you get a template with your logo swapped in.

Watch out for

Once the project is done, the freelancer moves on. Need to change your phone number, update your service area from Irvine to Newport Beach, or add a new photo? That's another invoice. Most Orange County small business owners end up with an outdated site because every small change costs money.

Option 3: Hire an agency

Cost: $2,000-$15,000 to build, retainer for ongoing management

A full agency brings a team: designer, developer, strategist, account manager. The work is polished and the process is smooth. But everything is a line item. SEO strategy is extra. Copywriting is extra. Updates are billed against a monthly retainer.

This makes sense for businesses doing $500k+ per year who need enterprise-level support. For most Orange County small businesses — whether you're in Fullerton, Santa Ana, or Huntington Beach — it is more than you need and more than you want to pay.

What most Orange County small businesses actually need

Most service businesses in Orange County — from Anaheim to Costa Mesa to Newport Beach — need a website that does four things well:

  • Loads fast on mobile — most of your customers search on their phone
  • Shows up when someone Googles your service in your city
  • Looks professional enough that people trust you before they call
  • Answers basic questions 24/7 so you don't lose leads while you're working

For an Orange County small business, this is often the difference between a customer booking you or booking your competitor. Your website is your first impression, and most people decide in seconds.

Questions to ask before you pay anyone

Before hiring anyone to build your website, get clear answers to these:

  • Is hosting included? If not, what's the monthly cost?
  • Who handles updates when my hours or services change?
  • Is local SEO setup included, or is that extra?
  • What happens if something breaks?
  • Is the site mobile-responsive, or do I need to request that separately?

The bottom line

For an Orange County small business, a professionally built and properly maintained website costs $500-$2,000 to launch and $50-$200 per month to keep running right. Anything cheaper usually means skipping something that will cost you leads — usually local SEO, mobile layout, or ongoing updates.
